RecoverX Support

RecoverX is a recovery and readiness app for Apple Watch users. It reads your Apple Health data on your device, scores your recovery with a transparent rule-based engine, and explains every number.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: email allenwu@umich.edu. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

  • How do I cancel my subscription? Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → RecoverX Pro → Cancel. It takes effect at the end of the current period. No tricks.
  • Where is my data stored? On your device. RecoverX has no server and never uploads your health data. See the privacy policy.
  • My score says "HRV not included" — why? Your Apple Watch records overnight HRV only while worn during sleep. If you charge your watch overnight, RecoverX tells you honestly instead of guessing.
  • How does the WHOOP import work? A free, one-time import of up to 90 days of your WHOOP history so your baseline starts warm. Tokens are discarded after the import.
