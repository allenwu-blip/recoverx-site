RecoverX Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

Overview

RecoverX ("we", "the app") is an iOS application that reads your health data to provide personalized recovery insights. This privacy policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and our commitments regarding your privacy.

Data We Access

RecoverX requests read-only access to the following Apple HealthKit data types:

  • Sleep analysis
  • Heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate
  • VO₂max
  • Workout records

All HealthKit access is subject to your explicit permission. You can revoke access at any time via iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

What We Do With Your Data

  • All health data is processed locally on your device. It is never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • We do not operate any servers or backend. There is no account, login, or cloud sync.
  • Anonymous usage telemetry (e.g., which screens you open, whether you tap the daily recovery score) is stored locally on your device only. You may optionally share this telemetry with the developer via the in-app "Export my usage data" button; this is fully opt-in and shows you exactly what is being shared before you send it.

Data Retention

All data created or accessed by RecoverX is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all RecoverX-specific data. HealthKit data itself is managed by Apple's Health app and is not affected by uninstalling RecoverX.

Third-Party Services

RecoverX does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app is fully self-contained.

Apple Services

RecoverX uses Apple HealthKit in compliance with Apple's HealthKit privacy guidelines. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the HealthKit framework and your Apple ID.

Children's Privacy

RecoverX is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Updates will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and posted at the same URL.
