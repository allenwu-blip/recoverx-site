RecoverX Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026 (applies to RecoverX 1.3.1 and later; the previous policy for 1.3 and earlier is summarized at the end).

The short version

  • Your health data stays on your iPhone. RecoverX reads Apple Health on the device, scores your recovery on the device, and never uploads your health readings.
  • Two optional features send something off the device, and only when you use them: the AI Coach on Pro (your typed questions and a coarse summary of your scores go to our proxy and then to Anthropic) and Crew (your display name and three daily scores are stored in your own iCloud and shared with the people you invite).
  • We use a crash reporter (Sentry) that receives technical crash details with health values removed.
  • There is no account, no login, no advertising, no data broker, and nothing is sold.

Data we read from Apple Health

RecoverX requests read-only access to these HealthKit types. All access is subject to your explicit permission, and you can revoke any type at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

  • Sleep analysis, heart rate, heart rate variability (Apple Watch reports SDNN), resting heart rate, VO₂max, and workout records. These produce your recovery, sleep and strain scores and your training history.
  • Steps, walking and running distance, and cycling distance. Read only inside the window of an effort the app has already detected from heart rate, to tell a walk from a run from a ride. Decline them and detection still works from heart rate alone; the app asks you the type instead of guessing.
  • Respiratory rate, wrist temperature during sleep, and one-minute heart rate recovery. Shown as your own trends against your own baseline. They do not change your recovery score, and nothing in RecoverX detects, diagnoses or screens for any condition.
  • Biological sex, read once. The published fitness-age reference tables are separated by sex. Decline it and RecoverX says it cannot compute the number rather than guessing one.
  • Date of birth, read once to pre-fill your age during setup. You can type your age instead and never grant this.
  • Menstrual flow, read only if you log it in Apple Health, and only to note where you are in your cycle next to your recovery score. It is never uploaded. Deny this one type, or turn cycle tracking off in Settings, and the app works normally without it.

RecoverX writes to Apple Health only when you ask it to: a workout you log by hand can be saved to Health as a workout record.

What stays on your device

Everything above, plus your score history, your settings, your sport picks, your daily check-ins and your usage telemetry (which screens you open, whether you tap the daily score) is stored on your iPhone. Usage telemetry is never sent automatically; the in-app "Export my usage data" button shows you exactly what would be shared before you choose to send it.

Uninstalling RecoverX removes all RecoverX data from the device. Your Apple Health data is managed by Apple's Health app and is not affected. Crew data you created in iCloud is described below.

AI Coach

The free coach runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device language model (iPhones that support Apple Intelligence). Nothing leaves the device.

On RecoverX Pro, the coach can answer from a cloud model. When you send a message on Pro, the app transmits:

  • the text you typed and the coach's previous replies in that conversation;
  • a coarse summary of today's scores: which of the five signals (sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, training load, how you said you feel) pushed the score up or down, and by roughly how much, on a small scale. It contains no raw Health readings, no heart-rate values, no sleep times and no timestamps; this is enforced by automated tests in the app;
  • the App Store's signed proof of your Pro purchase, so our proxy can confirm you are entitled to the cloud coach. It contains transaction and product identifiers and dates. It contains no name, email or payment details.

The request goes to a proxy we operate on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards it to Anthropic's API (the Claude model) and streams the answer back. Our proxy stores no message content. It keeps only counters, keyed by an App Store transaction identifier, so that each subscriber's daily and weekly message allowance and our monthly spending cap can be enforced; these counters expire on their own. Anthropic processes the text to generate the reply, deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, and does not use them to train its models by default (see Anthropic's retention policy). Cloudflare handles the request as our infrastructure provider under its privacy policy.

If you never send a message on Pro, none of this happens.

Crew

Crew is an optional leaderboard for a few friends. When you create or join a crew, RecoverX stores in your own iCloud private database (Apple CloudKit, under your Apple Account): a crew name, your display name, an anonymous member identifier, and each day's three scores (recovery, sleep, strain) as whole numbers. The crew's owner shares that record set with the members they invite, using iCloud sharing, so members can see one another's names and daily scores. Nothing else about you is shared: no Health readings, no history, no location.

We, the developers, do not have access to your Crew records; they live in your iCloud, governed by Apple's privacy policy. Leaving a crew removes your scores from the shared set; deleting a crew you own removes it for everyone. You can hide any member on your device, and report a member by email; the report contains the crew and member identifiers and the name shown, nothing else, and it is sent only when you send it.

Crash reporting

RecoverX uses Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) to receive crash and error reports so we can fix bugs. A report contains the app version, device model, iOS version, the code path that failed and a short trail of screen names. Health values, scores, message text and identifiers are stripped before anything is sent; this scrubbing is covered by automated tests. Sentry's handling of the data is described in Sentry's privacy policy. There is no advertising or behavioural analytics SDK in the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. As described above, the App Store's signed transaction is sent to our proxy only to verify Pro for the cloud coach. Offer codes and promo codes are redeemed through Apple.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke any Health type in iOS Settings; RecoverX adapts and tells you what it can no longer compute.
  • Use the free on-device coach and never send a message to the cloud coach.
  • Do not create or join a crew, or leave one at any time.
  • Delete the app to remove all local data.
  • Email us to ask what, if anything, our proxy holds under your transaction identifier; we can delete the counters on request.

Children

RecoverX is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

Updates are reflected by the date at the top and posted at this URL. When a new version of the app sends data somewhere new, this page changes before that version ships.

Versions 1.3 and earlier: the app had no cloud coach, no Crew and no active crash reporting; all processing was on the device, as the previous policy stated.
